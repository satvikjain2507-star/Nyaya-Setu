# Nyaya Setu: 3-minute live demo (TinyFish × Gemini)

**Opening line:** "Every AI chatbot answers from memory. Nyaya Setu goes to the official government website, live, and shows you it's doing it."

1. **Home (15s):** point at the *Live updates* feed. "These are this week's scheme and fraud-alert headlines, fetched live by TinyFish Search and translated into my language."
2. **Sahayak Live, centre tab (60s):** tap 🎤 and say in Hindi: *"मेरे पिताजी 72 साल के हैं, आयुष्मान कार्ड कैसे बनेगा?"*
   - The plan card shows which official site the agent will visit.
   - **The live browser window shows the TinyFish agent clicking through the government site.** The timeline lists each step.
   - The answer is spoken in Hindi, with key facts, next steps and an "Open official website" button.
3. **Scheme Navigator (40s):** ask *"I am a small farmer in Maharashtra, what help can I get?"*, then tap **✅ Verify live on official sites**. The green badge, official link and 🏛️ source chips confirm the answer against today's government pages.
4. **Document Simplifier → Scam Shield (30s):** type an SMS such as *"Your electricity will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 pm. Call officer 98xxxxxx immediately"*, then tap **🛡️ Scam check**. The verdict is 🚨 *Likely a SCAM*, with the 1930 helpline.
5. **Help Centres (15s):** shows real CSC, DLSA and police stations in your city, with Call and Directions buttons.

**Closing line:** "Gemini understands the citizen, and TinyFish acts on the live web. Together they give 1.4 billion people a government that speaks their language."

### Before going on stage
- Settings → **Test TinyFish connection** should show ✅.
- Run each demo once beforehand. Help centres and the news feed are cached, so they load instantly on stage.
- Have a backup ready: if venue Wi-Fi is slow, use mobile hotspot. Agent runs take 30–90 s, so talk over the live view while it runs.
